## 1. What 0.33 does

The kernel knows three applications by exact name. All of them are `exec=NONE`, and the executor is ABSENT:
- `app.counterview` requires `svc.counter`;
- `app.netstatus` and `app.browser` require `svc.netmon`. The browser's requirement is provisional.

With every 5 s status round the kernel prints, for each application, whether its prerequisites are AVAILABLE. These
are the same values the service status lines of that round report. The production kernel makes no request; its binary
does not even contain the launch-result line tags (S004 check S7).

The DIAGNOSTIC launchpad submits the fixed 17-request script. Every request:
- is admitted, parsed, recognized and then refused at its first failing gate;
- records the five facts separately;
- ends REFUSED or NOT_EXECUTED, with `executed=NO` and `process=NONE`.

No new public reason exists: every public reason is one of the 27 Appendix A codes (option A, D-ABR-022 Q2).

## 5. Limits and exceptions

1. **Nothing executes, by design.** The executor is ABSENT; the only execution value is NOT_EXECUTED; RUNNING and the
   0.4 states are reserved. The support and prerequisite facts never decide a public reason in 0.33, because
   authorization (gate 5) always refuses first (option A). They are reported on every line.
2. **The request id orders requests, and authenticates nothing** (Echo Recommendation 3). 0.33 has no actor, session
   or integrity binding.
3. **A clock-guard detection window, inherited from 0.32b.** In O003 the first status round after the 1 h TSC jump is
   evaluated 36 ms before the guard reports the jump (`uptime_ms=3603004` against `3603040`). That round honestly says
   `clock=OK` and counter AVAILABLE, and APPINFO follows it (SATISFIED). From the next round on, everything is
   UNKNOWN / UNSATISFIED on stale data. The launcher reports what the service status reports; the window is the 0.32b
   guard's.
4. **LAUNCHRESULT is a serial diagnostic, not an ExecutionReport or an audit record.** It has no audit store, no
   receipts and no decision id (P-17, P-46; design §12).
5. **Real execution in 0.4 is blocked by ratified rules** (P-09/P-30, P-17/P-46) until grants, an authority path and
   the audit store exist, or the owner ratifies an exception. The Appendix A gaps G-1 to G-3 are deferred (Q7).
6. **Q6 was done after the campaign, not in it.** It was added on the owner's request once the campaign and its
   supplement were closed, and recorded as supplement 2:
   - `kernel/hosttest/examples/launchpad.rs`, which is SIMULATED: the kernel's own launch.rs on the host, so you can
     type your own requests;
   - `tools/g033/launchpad_demo.py`, which boots the real diag-launchpad kernel and streams its console live.
   The kernel has no input path, so on the VM the launcher runs its compiled script only. The first viewer trial,
   DEV-DEMO1, was aborted by my own command (a closed pipe). Its folder is kept with a note; the viewer was then made
   robust.
7. **The DIAGNOSTIC builds of the old fault and memory paths compile with warnings** (early-fail, double-release,
   fault-ud, legacy-collision: unused or unreachable code in `apic.rs`, `cpu.rs`, `memory.rs`, `paging.rs` and the
   fault-injection lines of `main.rs`). These files and paths are unchanged by 0.33. The production, launchpad,
   application and service builds are warning-free.
8. **Inherited:**
   - the cooperative limit;
   - the stall misattribution (Y013);
   - a perfectly consistent lying service cannot be caught;
   - `reason=NONE` on some failure rows (D-ABR-019 Q3-B);
   - static checks are text and byte scans.
9. **Post-close files**, written after supplement 1 closed and not code under test:
   - `evidence_index_033.py`, `make_audit_033.py`, `check_checklist_033.py` (run: PASS);
   - this review, the operator checklist and the final patch notes;
   - the supplement-2 files: `examples/launchpad.rs`, `launchpad_demo.py`, `check_demo_033.py` and the `demo-*` modes
     of `campaign_033.py`.
